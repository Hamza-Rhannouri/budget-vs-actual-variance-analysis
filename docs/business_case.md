# Business Case

## Management question

The purpose of the analysis is to connect Budget vs Actual variance to the operating drivers behind profitability.

## Decision questions

**Performance:** Is actual revenue and profit tracking the plan?

**Drivers:** Which revenue and cost categories explain the variance?

**Exposure:** Which departments contribute most to unfavorable performance?

**Resilience:** How does profit respond when revenue or COGS assumptions change?

**Action:** Which operating levers deserve management attention?

The report is designed to move from **what happened → why it happened → what could happen → what to do next**.
